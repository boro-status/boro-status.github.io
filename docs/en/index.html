Elecard Boro Status Journal¶

This page is intended for publishing up-to-date information about Elecard Boro—the platform for monitoring video quality. Here you can learn about the current status of Elecard Boro services, check the incident history, and keep in touch with the planned maintenance works.

Here’s what you will find on this page:

  • Current services status — real-time information on the status of Boro components.

  • Incident history — record of failures, what caused them and what we did to fix them.

  • Announcements — notifications about planned maintenance works, updates, and changes in the operation of the platform.

If you encountered an issue not described on this page, contact the Elecard technical support team at tsup@elecard.com. We do our best to promptly publish information on any changes in the operation of our services so you can always be informed on the current state of affairs.
